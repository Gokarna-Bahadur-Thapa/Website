Gokarna Bahadur Thapa

Engineering Nepal’s energy surplus.

I am a mechanical engineer working on the demand models, feasibility studies and policy analysis that decide what Nepal does with the power it cannot yet use. My work is commissioned by the Alternative Energy Promotion Centre, UNDP, the Water and Energy Commission Secretariat and World Bank–supported programmes.

I build in LEAP, MAED and HOMER Pro, and publish peer-reviewed research on energy storage, industrial backup power and energy access under climate shocks.
